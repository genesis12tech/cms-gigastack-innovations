  2. 02DashboardEach card opens its area. The sidebar works everywhere.→
  3. 03Edit the homepageChange a field, save a draft, then publish with confirmation.→
  4. 04Add a serviceThe name fills the slug and SEO preview. Publish unlocks when complete.→

About this demo

Sample content

Leads, users, dates and figures are examples. Case studies are marked as samples until approved work is supplied.

Nothing is sent or stored

Saving, publishing, uploads and exports behave as they will in the CMS, but no server is involved. Users & Roles remembers changes in this browser only.

Designed for desktop

The CMS screens are designed at 1440px wide. Use the moon icon in any top bar to switch to dark mode.
